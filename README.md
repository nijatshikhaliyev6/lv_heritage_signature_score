# The Heritage Signature Score

**Does the form of a Louis Vuitton object carry the identity of the House independently of the Monogram?**

Independent research by Nijat Shikhaliyev, 2025.

## Overview

This project measures whether Louis Vuitton objects remain identifiable through structural form alone — rigidity, edge geometry, symmetry, hardware, and travel function — independent of the Monogram canvas or any surface ornament.

400 objects spanning 1854–2025 were rated on eight visual features. Two composite indices were built: **S8** (all eight features) and **S6** (structural features only, ornament excluded). A blind recognition survey of 30 silhouettes, each shown to an independent cohort of 100 respondents, tested whether either score predicts actual brand recognition.

## Key finding

The structural score (S6) correlates with recognition (r = +0.434, p = 0.017). The composite score including ornament (S8) does not (r = −0.076, p = 0.688). The effect strengthens among non-iconic models (r = +0.573, p = 0.013), indicating the result is not driven by fame alone.

## Methodology summary

- **Feature ratings:** Each object scored 1–5 on eight visual features against written definitions (Appendix A of the report)
- **Reference profile:** Median of 124 objects made 1854–1949
- **Scoring:** S = 1 − mean(|object − reference|) across selected features
- **Inter-rater reliability:** ICC(2,1) = 0.980 (S6), 0.986 (S8), based on independent re-coding of a 25% stratified sample
- **Recognition survey:** 30 black-silhouette stimuli (20 Louis Vuitton, 10 control brands), 100 independent respondents per stimulus, randomized presentation order

Full methodology, limitations, and statistical detail are in the accompanying PDF report.
